# Project Bootstrapper — VISION

## North Star
> Make "production-grade, AI-ready, day one" the default starting point for every new project.

## Picture
The gap between `create-next-app` and a real production AI app (auth, billing, RAG, MCP, observability, guardrails, agent-context files, CI/CD) is days of repetitive work. The vision: collapse it to minutes, with opinionated, secure, **agent-ready** scaffolds — so teams start at the finish line of setup.

## Honest strategic stance
Scaffolding is commoditized and hard to monetize standalone (D's market read — MARKET_RESEARCH.md). So the vision is deliberately modest: be the **best AI-app scaffolder**, used (1) internally across the lab's 7 products, (2) as a **ContextOS feature** ("start a new project with our golden path"), and (3) as a free brand/funnel asset. Not a company on its own.

## Phases
| Horizon | What |
|---------|------|
| Now | Internal scaffolder for the lab + OSS CLI |
| Y1 | Web UI + AI customization + templates (free/cheap) |
| Y2 | ContextOS feature; org golden-paths |
| Y3 | Enterprise standards/governance (only if pulled there) |

## Principles
1. Production-ready + secure by default (not toy starters).
2. **AI-agent-ready** out of the box (CLAUDE.md/AGENTS.md/mcp.json/evals/guardrails).
3. Opinionated (this lab's proven stack) but configurable.
4. Open-core; output is the user's, runs anywhere.
5. Don't over-invest — it's a feature/funnel, not the main bet.

## The bet
That a great AI-ready scaffolder is high-utility, cheap to build (it falls out of building the lab), and most valuable as internal leverage + a ContextOS feature — while being a nice free top-of-funnel and resume artifact.
